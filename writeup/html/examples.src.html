Q02

Conflict across versions

correct14.8 s · $0.170 · groundedness 1.0

QuestionThe prescriber added mealtime insulin to this Veteran's basal insulin, but the DPP-4 is still on the refill list. Our older VA handout says continue it. Should I flag that for the pharmacist?

Assuming VA: yes—flag it for pharmacist review. The current VA PBM 2025 clinician summary’s prandial-insulin flowchart says that when starting prandial insulin in addition to basal insulin, discontinue sulfonylurea, meglitinides, and DPP-4 inhibitor. The older 2020 VA clinician guide did say to continue DPP-4 when prandial insulin is added. Because these are VA clinical guidance documents answering the same question, use the more recent 2025 guidance. Don’t independently stop the refill—queue it for RPh/prescriber cleanup.

Judge, against the reference

correct

“Flags for pharmacist, cites 2025 guide's discontinue DPP-4 with prandial insulin, notes 2020 conflict, prefers newer guidance, advises against independent discontinuation.”

Citations

va_pbm_t2dm_clinicians_guide:p17va_pbm_t2dm_pharmacotherapy_2025:p9

Why it is here

Both documents are retrieved, the precedence rule (“more recent over older”) is applied and named, and the other document gets its one line, as REQUIREMENTS.md asks.

Q03

Multi-document, apparent conflict, dose vs coverage limit

correct21.6 s · $0.176 · groundedness 1.0

QuestionWe received Suboxone 8/2 mg films, four daily, quantity 120 for 30 days, for OUD. The VA guide says the usual maximum is 24 mg, but NYRx lists up to 32 mg. Is this automatically excessive—and could we substitute the buprenorphine patch on the VA formulary?

Assuming NY Medicaid/NYRx: not automatically excessive. Four Suboxone 8/2 mg films daily = 32/8 mg/day, qty 120/30 days. NYRx MAT F/Q/D allows four 8/2 mg films/day, max 120/30 days, not to exceed 32/8 mg/day; this is in effect on 10/18/2026. VA’s OUD guide gives a usual max 24 mg/day, but also says higher doses may be used in some cases with careful monitoring and documented rationale.

Do not substitute a buprenorphine patch for OUD: the VA patch is formulary, but VA guidance describes patches for chronic pain, not as an OUD equivalent to Suboxone film.

Judge, against the reference

correct

“Correctly states 32/8 mg/day fits the NYRx limit, the VA 24 mg usual max permits higher doses with monitoring, and the patch is a chronic-pain product, not an OUD substitute.”

Citations

ny_medicaid_pdl:p76va_national_formulary_2026_09:r304va_pbm_bup_chronic_pain:p2va_pbm_bup_oud:p1va_pbm_bup_oud:p2

Why it is here

Four documents and a formulary spreadsheet row (r304, from lookup_va_formulary) in one answer, with the coverage limit kept apart from the clinical “usual maximum”. The slowest golden answer at 21.6 s, inside the 30 s limit.

Q08

Structured data: claim code and conditional policy

correct7.2 s · $0.054 · groundedness 0.833

QuestionA new LTC resident arrived without meds, and their first fill rejects with 01642. Which override applies?

Assuming NY Medicaid: use the New Patient Processing (NP) override for Early Fill Overuse reject 01642 if this is an LTC servicing pharmacy and the resident was newly admitted without meds.

Bill:

  1. 439-E4 Reason for Service Code = NP
  2. 420-DK Submission Clarification Code = 18 (LTC Patient Admit/Readmit Indicator)

This applies only when the claim is the first dispensing after recent LTC admission for the same drug/strength/directions and the eligibility response shows the applicable LTC facility indicator. Day supply is limited to 30 days unless short-cycle billing applies.

Judge, against the reference

correct

“Gives NP in 439-E4 plus SCC 18, states LTC pharmacy, recent admission with LTC eligibility indicator, first dispensing conditions, and the 30-day limit.”

Q09

Silence vs absence

correct12.8 s · $0.225 · groundedness 1.0

QuestionAfter prescriber approval, what code clears a Part D 90-MME alert? Do our documents specify one?

For Medicare Part D, our documents do not specify a universal code to clear the 90-MME opioid care-coordination alert after prescriber approval. CMS says to consult/confirm intent with the prescriber, document the discussion, and “submit the appropriate override code,” with POS override mechanisms available through the plan. So use the plan/PBM’s reject messaging or help desk instructions for the actual SCC/override values.

Also, 569 appears in the DMP guidance as a reject/notice-related code, not as the documented code to clear the 90-MME alert.

Judge, against the reference

correct

“States no code specified, describes consult/document/submit override process, directs to plan instructions; 569 clarification doesn't contradict reference.”

Citations

cms_partd_dmp_guidance_2019:p37cms_partd_opioid_pharmacists_2022:p1

Why it is here

The corpus covers the topic but not the detail. The assistant says so, gives what the documents do say, and does not invent a code.

Q13

Prior auth process: a weak answer

partial9.1 s · $0.052 · groundedness 1.0

QuestionHow do I start a PA for a non-preferred drug for an NY Medicaid pt: who do I contact and what form?

Assuming NY Medicaid/NYRx: for a non-preferred PDL drug, the PA must be submitted by the prescriber or the prescriber’s authorized agent—a dispensing pharmacist is not an authorized agent unless employed by the prescriber’s office.

  1. Phone PA: call the NYRx Clinical Call Center: 1-877-309-9493.
  2. ePA: use CoverMyMeds; the pharmacy may start the case, but it routes to the prescriber to complete/submit.
  3. Fax form: download the NYRx PA fax form at https://newyork.fhsc.com/providers/PA_forms.asp.
  4. If urgent and prescriber can’t be reached, pharmacist may request up to a 72-hour emergency supply PA.

Judge, against the reference

partial

“Gives prescriber-only rule, call center number, CoverMyMeds, and 72-hour supply, but omits PAXpress and gives a different fax-form URL instead of the Standardized PDF.”

What the reference has that the answer lacks

PAXpress; the standardized fax form.

Citations

ny_medicaid_pdl:p1ny_medicaid_pharmacy_policy:p15ny_medicaid_pharmacy_policy:p16

why it failsretrieved but answered wrong

Failure class “retrieved but answered wrong” (results.csv, Q13). The pages naming PAXpress and the standardized fax form were returned by the search and every claim in the answer is supported (groundedness 1.0), but the model stopped after one lookup of the six allowed and summarized the PA channels incompletely.

This is the core use case (“what's the prior auth process?”), so it is the miss that matters most.

how it would be fixeda prompt rule for channel questions

Two mechanisms were built and measured on 2026-10-18 as config flags (agent.min_lookups, agent.completeness_pass): refusing an answered submit after a single lookup once, and one extra model turn that adds omitted channels, limits and dates from the evidence already in context. They raised groundedness (0.937 → 0.942), halved unsupported claims (2.5% → 1.2%) and fixed Q18, but left Q13 partial and added 8 s of median latency, so they are off in production (reports/evals/v2_complete_2026-10-18_1819; EVALUATION.md §4.5).

The remaining lever is a prompt rule for “who do I contact / what form” questions: enumerate every channel the page lists before submitting.

V01

Flowchart branch: correct answer, wrong page cited

correct · groundedness 0.06.6 s · $0.040 · groundedness 0.0

QuestionPer the VA/DoD diabetes guideline's T2DM management algorithm, a patient has no ASCVD, nephropathy or heart failure and is not at their glycemic target. What is the next step?

Per the VA/DoD T2DM management algorithm: if the patient has no established ASCVD/high ASCVD risk, no diabetic nephropathy, and no HF, skip the GLP-1 RA/SGLT-2 comorbidity-driven step and assess glycemic target. If the patient is not at target, the next step is to consider additional glucose-lowering medications until the target A1C range is achieved, selecting the agent based on efficacy and overall risk–benefit ratio; the algorithm points to CPG Appendix B for medication selection support.

Judge, against the reference

correct

“Matches reference: skips GLP-1/SGLT-2 step, then add medications until target A1C based on efficacy and risk-benefit, citing Appendix B.”

Groundedness judge

0.0: “The cited page has only the guideline title, the ‘Module A: T2DM Management’ heading and an abbreviation list. It contains none of the algorithm's decision steps.”

Citations

va_dod_cpg_diabetes_2023:p21

why it is weakthe judge reads the page's text, not the figure

The flowchart is a raster image on page 21; the vision pass read it into a figure record (va_dod_cpg_diabetes_2023:p21:f0) and the citation points at that page, but the judge is given the page's extracted text, which for an image page is just the heading. The golden set's gold pages are the appendix (p145–146) where the same algorithm is printed as text; the agent never retrieved those. All five “answered right but cited wrong” cases in the visual run are this pattern (v2_visual_2026-10-18_0916/summary.md).

how it would be fixedmeasurement first, then the product

(1) In measurement, give the groundedness judge the figure interpretation for any cited page that carries a figure (scripts/run_eval.py, src/pharmassist/eval/judge.py), which would move the visual groundedness (0.614) without changing any answer.

(2) In the product, cite the figure explicitly (crop link plus “figure on p21”; the UI's FigureNote already does the first part) and add the appendix page as a second citation.

why it failsingested but not retrieved
